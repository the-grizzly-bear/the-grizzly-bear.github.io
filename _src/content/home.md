- **[HackTheBox](/htb/index.html)** — completed machines, standalone challenges, and the Hack The Boo seasonal events.
- **[Flare-on Challenges](/flareon/index.html)** — reverse-engineering writeups from the annual Flare-on contest.
- **[Other](/other/index.html)** — the Blue Team Field Manual, CSO CTFs, TryHackMe, SANS Holiday Hack, and lab exercises.
- **[Cyber Security Notes](/notes/index.html)** — curated links, threat intel, threat hunting, and incident-response reference notes.

> Note: writeups from before 2024 are often just screenshots rather than detailed notes.

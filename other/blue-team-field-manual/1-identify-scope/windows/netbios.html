NETBIOS

Basic nbtstat scan:

C:\> nbtstat -A <IP ADDRESS>

Cached NetBIOS info on localhost:

C:\> nbtstat -c

Script loop scan:

C:\> for /L %I in (1,1,254) do nbstat -An 192.168.1.%I

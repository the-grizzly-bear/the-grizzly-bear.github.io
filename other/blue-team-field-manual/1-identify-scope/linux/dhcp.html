DHCP

View DHCP lease logs:
Red Hat 3:

cat /var/lib/dhcpd/dhcpd.leases

Ubuntu:

grep -Ei 'dhcp' /var/log/syslog.1

Ubuntu DHCP logs:

tail -f dhcpd.log
